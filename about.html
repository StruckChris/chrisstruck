Biography

About Chris Struck

Christopher M. Struck, also known as Chris Struck and C. M. Struck, is an independent mathematics researcher, author, singer, and software developer. His Nonlinear Calculus research develops the Matrix of Error across fluid dynamics, relativity, Lebesgue integration, rigidity, and multibody mechanics. This collection brings together his nine Zenodo preprints: Paper 8 connects the shared methods, and Paper 9 develops their mathematical connections and worked applications.

His professional background spans institutional finance, investment due diligence, financial reporting, risk governance, and applied data science. His experience includes accounting and reporting at Mizuho Americas, operational and product-risk strategy at Morgan Stanley Wealth Management, institutional RFP and due-diligence workflows at GoldenTree Asset Management, financial-services assurance at Ernst & Young, and machine-learning work for community banking at 2Novas. His independent technical work includes software development and game design for a 3d Tactical RPG and an FPS campaign.

He holds a Master of Applied Data Science from the University of Michigan, a Master of Accounting from University of Michigan's Ross Business School, and a Bachelor of Business Administration double major in both accounting and finance from the University of Wisconsin–Milwaukee.

Writing and music have developed alongside his analytical work. His published novels, though not currently in print, include Kennig & Gold, 8: A Song for the Peach Tree In My Master’s Garden, and The Sun Never Set. He is the singer behind 11th Angel on Spotify and the creator of True Legends of Frost, a steampunk fantasy series on YouTube.
